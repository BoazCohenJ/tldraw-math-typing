# Privacy policy — Math Typing for tldraw

_Last updated: 2026-11-25_

Math Typing for tldraw does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data or usage data.

- **What it reads:** the text you type into text boxes on tldraw.com, only on your own computer, to
  replace shortcuts with symbols and to run the commands you type (such as `/calc`). This never leaves
  your browser.
- **What it stores:** your Math Typing settings and your own shortcuts, in your browser's extension
  storage on your computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests of its own. It runs only on tldraw.com pages.
- **Third parties:** none. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- **What it changes:** the text and shapes on the tldraw board you are editing, when you type a
  shortcut or a command. tldraw itself saves your board as it always does.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/yotam1001/tldraw-math-typing/issues.
